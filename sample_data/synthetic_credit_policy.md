# Synthetic SME Credit Policy

This is fictional test content and must not be used for real lending decisions.

## Minimum documents

- Completed application form
- Two years of financial statements
- Six months of operating bank statements
- Current trade license
- Tax return acknowledgement
- Accounts receivable and inventory ageing reports

## Illustrative review thresholds

- Debt service coverage ratio should be at least 1.25x.
- Total debt to EBITDA should not exceed 3.50x.
- Customer concentration above 30% requires enhanced review.
- Missing or inconsistent source documents require human review.

## Approval

The AI may summarize and flag information. It may not approve, reject, or price a real credit facility.
